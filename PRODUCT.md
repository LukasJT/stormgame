# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite, TypeScript, SVG map and HTML controls, as specified in the build brief.

## Product Purpose

Play as severe weather on a fictional regional map. Pick a starting point, steer, invest in storm upgrades, and compare the final death toll and damage score. The first milestone proves one full hurricane run and replay.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Pausable time, selectable coastal origins, offshore growth, local warnings and shelter, and a live death toll. Support desktop, keyboard and touch. Keep the simulation reproducible from the forecast seed.
