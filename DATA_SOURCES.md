# Map and population sources

## Satellite image

`public/blue-marble.jpg` is the July 2004 global base map from [NASA Earth Observatory's Blue Marble Next Generation](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-map/), at 5,400 × 2,700 pixels. Closer zooms overlay EPSG:4326 Blue Marble Next Generation tiles from [NASA GIBS](https://nasa-gibs.github.io/gibs-api-docs/access-basics/). The bundled map stays visible if a tile is unavailable. The colored starting zones are authored gameplay regions.

`src/data/cities.json` contains 7,332 places from the [Natural Earth 1:10m Populated Places Simple layer](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/v5.1.2/geojson/ne_10m_populated_places_simple.geojson) and 305 additional Canadian places from [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/export/), licensed CC BY 4.0. The city list also includes Fort Worth using [FCC community coordinates](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title47-vol4/pdf/CFR-2025-title47-vol4-sec76-53.pdf) and its 2020 U.S. Census population. Rows include the name, position, population estimate, country code, and Canadian or U.S. province or state when available. Local map labels use nearby places within 55 km and respect country and subdivision boundaries. Standalone tornado names use the nearest mapped city to touchdown. Gameplay populations are calculated separately.

## Population and housing

- Custom starts and country inspection use simplified [Natural Earth 1:50m country boundaries](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/v5.1.2/geojson/ne_50m_admin_0_countries.geojson). National U.S. and Canadian counts are from [U.S. Census QuickFacts](https://www.census.gov/quickfacts/fact/table/US/PST045224) and the [2021 Statistics Canada census](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=9810000101). Other country counts use Tornado Simulator or Natural Earth estimates.
- New York City land and water use Department of City Planning borough geometry clipped to the shoreline at mean high tide. The water-inclusive borough boundary limits where the city geometry overrides the base map. New Jersey land on the other side of the harbor uses NJDEP's coastal municipalities. The shoreline layers are generalized to roughly 10 m. [Clipped NYC shoreline](https://services5.arcgis.com/GfwWNkhOj9bNBqoJ/ArcGIS/rest/services/v_NYC_Borough_Boundary/FeatureServer/0) · [Water-inclusive extent](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/City-Government/Borough-Boundaries/wh2p-dxnf) · [New Jersey coastal municipalities](https://mapsdep.nj.gov/arcgis/rest/services/Features/Government/MapServer/19)
- Select a country to load its simplified [Natural Earth administrative outlines](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/10m-admin-1-states-provinces/). Regional population estimates use the [GeoNames CC BY 4.0 gazetteer](https://www.geonames.org/export/) and cover 4,403 of the 4,596 bundled regions. Population dates vary. Missing figures show as unavailable. Injured counts represent living injured residents in the current game. Injuries and deaths are assigned to a region by the modeled zone's location.
- Hurricane categories follow the [National Hurricane Center's Saffir–Simpson Hurricane Wind Scale](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/aboutsshws.php). Width, winds, exposure and impacts are game-model readings.
- `src/data/tornado-population.json` reuses the 177 national and 288 Canadian census-division records embedded in [Tornado Simulator](https://www.tornadosimulator.net/simulator/). The original annotations attribute national totals and densities to UN population and CIA area tables, and Canadian figures to the Statistics Canada 2021 Census. The source project's MIT notice is included at `public/TornadoSimulator-LICENSE.txt`.
- Moving storm tracks use mapped census division densities for Toronto, Peel, York, Durham, and Halton. The boundaries come from [Statistics Canada’s 2021 Census Division layer for Ontario](https://services.arcgis.com/IBZpB6oNXY4mCIej/ArcGIS/rest/services/Ontario_Census_Divisions_2021/FeatureServer/0).
- Outside those mapped GTA divisions, Canada and the U.S. use nearby census division and county estimates from [Tornado Simulator’s census data](https://github.com/LukasJT/TornadoSimulator). The source includes representative coordinates for 288 Canadian divisions and 3,144 U.S. counties. Matches use those coordinates as an approximation where detailed boundaries are unavailable. Existing named scenarios retain their explicit census records.
- Elsewhere, a populated administrative region supplies population density from its bundled population estimate divided by its mapped area. These estimates use the housing assumption of 0.4 homes per person. If a local match is unavailable, the game uses the national density when available.
- `src/data/us-counties.json` stores population and housing for 24 modeled counties from [2022 ACS five-year tables B01003 and B25001](https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/summary_file/2022/table-based-SF/data/5YRData/). The densities divide those counts by land area from the [2022 Census county Gazetteer](https://www2.census.gov/geo/docs/maps-data/data/gazetteer/2022_Gazetteer/).

The thunderstorm upgrade paths draw on [NSSL’s thunderstorm types](https://www.nssl.noaa.gov/education/svrwx101/thunderstorms/types/). The model abstracts rotating updrafts, cold-pool outflow, and development of new cells into competing upgrade paths. Wildfire's wind and moisture conditions draw on the [National Wildfire Coordinating Group's fire weather guide](https://www.nwcg.gov/publications/pms425-1/6-general-winds). Conditions and effects are tuned for play.

Blizzard recommendations use broad geographic zones modeled after [National Weather Service winter storm tracks](https://www.weather.gov/ctp/SnowStormTypes) and [NOAA winter forecasting examples](https://www.nssl.noaa.gov/education/svrwx101/winter/forecasting/).

Thunderstorm and funnel tracks use game-tuned movement and modeled populations within small map cells. EF ratings are simulated and take inspiration from the [National Weather Service Enhanced Fujita Scale](https://www.weather.gov/oun/efscale). Storm Records created before dollar scores retain the lower bound represented by their original score.

Stormwake adapts [Tornado Simulator's](https://www.tornadosimulator.net/simulator/) swept-area method, housing fragility curves, shelter-specific fatality rates, and injury ratio for incremental simulation. The game retains existing shelter and warning mechanics and applies severity conversions for hazards besides tornadoes. EF0 has no modeled deaths and reduced structural losses for tornadoes. The dollar total reflects modeled property damage.

Tornado upgrades use meteorological terms for gameplay effects. Environmental changes are seeded and temperature exposure is approximated with latitude. Hail and rainfall upgrades contribute modeled property losses. Rain wrapping changes spotter detection. [NWS tornado development guide](https://www.weather.gov/spotterguide/develop) · [NWS tornado and rain-wrapping guidance](https://www.weather.gov/ffc/torntext)

The shaded tornado regions are approximate gameplay polygons inspired by the supplied map and published climatology. They affect the rate of inflow depletion and weakening. Town impacts are estimated using the simulated swath, property damage, and the proximity and size of named settlements. [NOAA tornado basics](https://www.nssl.noaa.gov/education/svrwx101/tornadoes/) · [Global tornado environments study](https://www.nssl.noaa.gov/users/brooks/public_html/papers/ECSS2002proof.pdf)

The Dispatch compares simulated deaths with historical single-tornado benchmarks for the United States (695), Canada (28), and the world (about 1,300). U.S. and Canadian comparisons count casualties in the respective country. [NWS Tri-State tornado](https://www.weather.gov/pah/1925tornado) · [Government of Canada tornado history](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/policing/emergencies/preparedness/get-prepared/hazards-emergencies/tornados.html) · [WMO extremes table](https://wmo.int/sites/default/files/2025-07/Table_Records_25Jul2025.pdf)
- A land zone represents up to 300 square miles, capped by the population of its source county or division. National averages are used for other regions. These are inputs for game scores and warning behavior. They are area estimates, so a named city marker represents its modeled surrounding area.
# Weather signatures

- **Tropical cyclone reflectivity.** NOAA/NWS New Orleans archived WSR-88D reflectivity of Katrina, August 29, 2005. [Event archive](https://www.weather.gov/lix/katrina_anniversary)
- **Supercell reflectivity.** The moving hook and rain core are modeled from the precipitation structure described by NWS. The classic Twin Lakes reflectivity from the May 3, 1999 Oklahoma tornado outbreak served as a visual reference. [NWS radar guide](https://www.weather.gov/mlb/Doppler_Dual_Pol_Weather_Radar) · [NSSL case study](https://www.nssl.noaa.gov/tools/decision/cases/990503/StormA.html)
- **Snowband reflectivity.** NOAA/NWS Northern Indiana lake-effect and snow squall frames from November 2022, and NOAA/NWS composite reflectivity of the January 2016 nor’easter. [Lake effect and squalls](https://www.weather.gov/iwx/November2022-LES) · [Nor’easter](https://www.weather.gov/ctp/01222016_blizzard)
- **Cell and squall-line reflectivity.** NOAA/NWS Louisville example radar products. [Radar examples](https://www.weather.gov/lmk/nws_radar_SVR-images)
- **Major lakes.** Natural Earth 1:110m lake polygons, used alongside the coastline to distinguish water starts, including the Great Lakes. [Natural Earth lakes](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/110m-physical-vectors/)

The radar assets are color-keyed excerpts of archived reflectivity and are positioned, scaled, and sometimes rotated by the simulation. They illustrate representative signatures. Tornado swaths use the game's modeled wind estimates, with soil scouring approximated from strength, width, and a seeded ground-susceptibility proxy. [NWS EF scale](https://www.weather.gov/oun/efscale) · [NHC inland weakening](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/outreach/presentations/2011_InlandWindsTornadoes_Brennan.pdf)

Blizzard storm-total colors are adapted from [NWS Albany snowfall verification maps](https://www.weather.gov/media/aly/stormbuster/Spring14SB.pdf). Accumulations come from the game model and are interpolated between grid samples.
